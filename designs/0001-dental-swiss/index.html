What we do

  1. Check-ups & hygiene

    A full examination, scale and polish, and honest advice about what can wait. Every six months for most people, every twelve for some.

  2. Fillings & repairs

    White fillings matched to your tooth. Most are finished in one visit, and we numb the area properly before we begin.

  3. Crowns & bridges

    Made by a lab twenty minutes away, fitted in two visits. We show you the shade match in daylight, not under the surgery lamp.

  4. Children & nervous adults

    Longer slots, a slower pace, and a hand signal that always means stop. No one is ever told they are being silly.
