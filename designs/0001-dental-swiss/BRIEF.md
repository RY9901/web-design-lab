## The brand

**Harbourline Dental** is an invented two-dentist family practice on a quiet street above a
ferry terminal. It serves local households, from toddlers' first check-ups to dentures. The
voice is calm, exact and unfussy: short sentences, no exclamation marks, no stock-photo
smiles. The Swiss aesthetic means a strict grid, one sans-serif family, flush-left ragged-right
text, big numerals, thin rules, and a palette of off-white, near-black and a single signal red.

Suggested tokens (you may change them, but keep to three colours): paper `#f4f2ee`,
ink `#111111`, signal `#d8281e`.

### Treatments (four items in a row/grid)
Section title: **What we do**
1. **Check-ups & hygiene** — A full examination, scale and polish, and honest advice about
   what can wait. Every six months for most people, every twelve for some.
2. **Fillings & repairs** — White fillings matched to your tooth. Most are finished in one
   visit, and we numb the area properly before we begin.
3. **Crowns & bridges** — Made by a lab twenty minutes away, fitted in two visits. We show
   you the shade match in daylight, not under the surgery lamp.
4. **Children & nervous adults** — Longer slots, a slower pace, and a hand signal that always
   means stop. No one is ever told they are being silly.
